How to Play

🍬 Swap & Match: Match 3 or more candies of the same color in a row or column to pop them!

⚡ Line Blaster (4-Match): Clears an entire row or column when matched.

🌈 Rainbow Bomb (5-Match): Match 5 in a row to get a Rainbow Bomb. Swap it with any candy to explode ALL candies of that color!

🔨 Boosters: Use the Hammer to crush single tiles, Shuffle to reset the board, or Color Bomb to delete a color!

🎯 Modes: Complete the target before running out of moves, race the clock in Timed Rush, or relax in Endless Mode.
